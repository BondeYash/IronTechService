ABOUT US

The Structural Steel Detailing Company

We specialize in detailing services for both structural steel and miscellaneous steel structures. Our team of professional steel detailers is highly trained and skilled in providing clean, accurate, and comprehensive structural detailing services on schedule. We pride ourselves on exceeding our customer’s expectations, needs, and demands by delivering exceptional services that are unmatched in the industry.

At Irontech Detailing, we follow customized fabrication standards and project scopes to ensure that we meet our client’s unique requirements. We adhere to guidelines set by AISC, NISD, and OSHA to produce structures of the future that are not only aesthetically pleasing but also safe and reliable. We utilize the latest technology, such as SDS/2, to ensure efficiency and accuracy in our projects.

Our in-house steel detailing staff is experienced and seasoned in the industry, and we take pride in providing exceptional services to our clients. We understand the importance of delivering a quality product at competitive rates, while meeting demanding project schedules. We believe in creating intelligent instruction that guides the procurement, fabrication, and erection of steel. Our focus is on reducing cost and increasing efficiency in the fabrication shop and in steel erection.

Our mission is to provide innovative and high-quality steel detailing services that meet and exceed our clients' expectations. We believe that our attention to detail, dedication to quality, and commitment to safety set us apart from other steel detailing companies.
Thank you for considering Irontech Detailing Services Pvt Ltd for your structural steel detailing needs. We look forward to working with you and providing exceptional services that will bring your projects to life.
